What to check before ordering

HubBuy shoe finds and streetwear sizing

For a sneaker shortlist, save the exact model and colorway. Compare the sneaker size chart with your foot length in centimeters and ask for an insole measurement if you are between sizes. For hoodie fit or T-shirt sizing, lay a piece you own flat and measure chest width, shoulder width and body length.

Use sleeve length to compare jacket fit, and check waist and inseam for pants and shorts. For a matching set, confirm whether the top and bottom can be selected separately. For a jersey, compare chest width over the layer you intend to wear underneath.

HubBuy product links and variant checks

Save the original marketplace link and item ID for a Weidian, Taobao or 1688 listing. When you reopen a saved link, check the current seller listing and the size and color selected in the variant menu. A gallery image may show a different variant from the one selected.

For a baseball cap, check the head circumference range and strap closure. Compare bag dimensions and strap drop against what you carry. For scarves and jewelry, check scarf dimensions, fiber content, earring dimensions and clasp type.

Warehouse QC photos

Quality-control photos are inspection images. Ask for photos of the specific warehouse item where available. For sneakers, request both outsole views and a heel comparison. For clothing, look at stitching and the care label. For a bag, ask to see the zipper, hardware and lining.

If color or finish affects your decision, request a photo under neutral lighting. Check the seller’s return window and any inspection or return fees while you still have time to act.

International shipping estimate

For a useful landed-cost estimate, enter your destination country and postal code. Ask for actual parcel weight and packed dimensions. A carrier may calculate the shipping rate using dimensional weight, especially if you keep bulky shoe boxes.

Compare quotes in the same currency, such as USD for the United States, GBP for the United Kingdom or EUR for euro-area destinations. Check which amounts cover domestic delivery, agent service fees, international shipping and any destination duties or taxes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a HubBuy spreadsheet?

A HubBuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference that groups product links and may include notes about sizes, variants or quality-control photos. This independent guide explains how to evaluate those entries and links to CAFANS categories.

How do I check if a spreadsheet product link is still useful?

Open the current listing and match the product model, selected color and size. If an older agent link fails, look for the original marketplace name and item ID in your saved notes.

Can QC photos confirm authenticity?

QC photos can show visible details such as seams, labels and damage. Authenticity requires separate evidence, such as a verifiable purchase record from an authorized seller.

Does the listed item price include shipping?

Check what the quote includes. Item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and destination charges can appear as separate amounts.
